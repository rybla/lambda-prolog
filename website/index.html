λProlog

A Haskell interpreter for the higher-order hereditary Harrop fragment of λProlog: simply-typed λ-terms, pattern unification, and uniform proof search with pi and =>.

What this is

Hereditary Harrop formulas extend Horn clauses with implication and universal quantification in goals. Operationally that means a program is not a flat clause store: proving D => G installs D for the duration of G, and proving pi x\ G introduces a fresh eigenvariable. Nested programs and signatures are the hierarchical structure unique to this language.
